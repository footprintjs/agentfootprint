**The results layer compares what each call read with what it asked.** Under `.time()`, a call whose read is not the window asked about is recorded and said: the result's declared `period.queried` (read back with the tool's `granularity`, else 1 ms), a widened fill, or a look-back that ran late after a pause is compared with the range the call asked for — or, for a window the model chose, with the person's window — and the call's `period` row gains `differs: { missing, extra }` (asked but not read; read but not asked), `shifted`, `beyondRetention` and `partlyBeyondRetention`. The answer's standing folds two new reasons, `period-differs-from-asked` (a wider read too, unless the result declares it read exactly what was asked) and `period-beyond-retention`, both "not sure"; `agentfootprint.findings.period` names the checks in `timeChecks` (never a range); and `.limitsTravelWithTheAnswer()` prints one line per such call under `Period`, in the person's zone, plus a `Clocks` section naming each dataset whose declared time axis has a zone (a new `source-clock` row) and saying when two sources' clocks differ — a label only. A closed end read as open is not a difference, nor is a look-back the tool read LATER by no more than its step (the tool's clock ran a little past the turn's) — a read moved earlier, or any other read moved at all, keeps its `missing`, and a period's offset (`Z` or `-07:00`) is never read as a clock. Without `.time()` every row, event and line is unchanged. The model reads the same conclusion before it answers: under `.time()`, once a call's read differs from what it asked (or the time asked about is older than the source keeps, or two sources' clocks differ), every later request of the turn ends with one line naming what was asked and what was read, in the person's zone, and what an answer states — a turn whose reads match serves no line.
