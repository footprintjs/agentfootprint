**An answer check that found a contradiction is reported `failed`, even when other checks could not reach a verdict.** Before, one `unreachable` row made the whole `.answerValidation()` report `unverified`, so a contradiction the check had actually found read as "could not tell" — in the report and in the answer's standing. Now a `checked-fail` row makes the report `failed`, and its `reason` is still `unreachable-checks`, so the rows that could not be compared stay visible. The standing names both: `answer-check-failed` and `check-unreachable`. Enforce mode withholds the answer either way; only the label changes. A contradiction one of the agent's own rows found (`agentChecks`) is `failed` even when your validator throws, times out or returns something invalid — those rows never depended on it — and the reason says what else happened. Otherwise, every other reason (a timeout, a validator error, an incomplete tool-call list) still makes the report `unverified`, because then nothing your check returned is a verdict.
