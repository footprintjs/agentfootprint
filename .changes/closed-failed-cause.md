**Saved-history cause tools can admit a recorded failed native prefix.** After FootPrint's public stored-history reader validates a supported closed native root prefix, the existing cause and impact tools retain its failed outcome, exact selected invocation and explicit limitations. Required channel, source ownership and control checks remain in force. Live snapshots and failed histories without that proof remain incomplete; neither a closed prefix nor a storage acknowledgement establishes producer identity, semantic cause, remote cancellation or whole-workflow delivery.
