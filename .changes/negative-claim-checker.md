A typed claim cites the library-minted result identities of the reads it rests on and is established only from those reads, with a library-written statement that quotes every text the tool supplied; a prose claim found by a fixed, versioned and hashed grammar is only noted. The answer bench now reads its hedge and attribution phrases, and the non-existence and completeness grammar, from this folder. `bench/negative/` replays the grammar over recorded replies for free.
