**`accountForAnswer` stays within its stated size on any saved recording.** A pointer copied an event's `runtimeStageId` and a history message's `toolCallId` from the record whole, and the standing fact listed one pointer per row the standing rests on. One long id, repeated on every pointer into its event, could turn a recording of a few kilobytes into an account of megabytes, past the 128 KB the account promises. Both ids are now cut at 200 characters in every pointer, as the account's other ids are, and the standing fact lists at most 50 pointers; its lines still count every row. Accounts of ordinary records are unchanged; a standing that rests on more than 50 rows lists the first 50.
