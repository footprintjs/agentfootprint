**The hosted `answer-account` operation explains a recording saved in parts.** A ref naming a `recording/run-index` is explained from the whole recording read back with `openSavedRecording(…).readWhole()`, so the answer equals the one for the same run filed whole, including under `reactMode: 'dynamic-grouped'`, where the account's omission check reads the subflow results. `maxRecordingBytes` bounds the whole recording's plain bytes, as it does a whole recording's; any part missing or changed is the one not-found, never a partial account. Before a cached answer is served, every part is head-checked against the index, so an answer is never served for a recording that is no longer whole in the store. On a miss the door facts are one `artifacts.resolved` per part read, naming the part. `SavedRecording` gains `checkParts()`, the heads-only presence check. Alongside: a parts filing on a store that does not state its budget is named once per store in dev mode, and the readers that take a whole recording (`epochLocations`, `openRecording`, `assembleTrajectory`, `contextLedger().recordRun`) say how to pass one saved in parts.
