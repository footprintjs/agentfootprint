# Refund policy

This document is the authority on refunds. Where a support macro and this
document disagree, this document wins.

## Refund timing

Refunds are processed within 3 business days of approval. The money is returned
to the original payment method. Your bank may take a further 2-5 business days
to show it, which is outside our control and should be said plainly to the
customer rather than promised away.

## Eligibility

Purchases are refundable within 30 days of the original purchase date. Digital
goods that have been downloaded are not refundable once accessed, because the
value has already been delivered.

## Partial refunds

An annual plan cancelled mid-term is refunded pro rata, rounded to the nearest
whole month in the customer's favour.
