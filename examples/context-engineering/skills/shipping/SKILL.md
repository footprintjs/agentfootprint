# Shipping

Ask for the tracking number first; without it every answer is a guess.

A parcel is only "lost" after 10 business days past its estimated delivery
date. Before that, say it is delayed and offer to re-check tomorrow.

Address changes are possible only while the parcel is still at the origin
depot. After that, the customer must refuse delivery and reorder.
