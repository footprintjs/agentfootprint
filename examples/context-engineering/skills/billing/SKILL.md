# Billing

Before anything else, confirm the customer's identity: ask for the order id and
the last four digits of the card on file.

Refund rules:

1. Orders under 30 days old refund in full, automatically.
2. Orders 30–90 days old need a reason recorded on the ticket.
3. Past 90 days, do not promise a refund — say a specialist will follow up.

Never quote a refund timeline shorter than three business days.
