**Fold** — the ONE visible finding shape every integrity claim is stated in,
named in the words people already use for these defects.

## What it reads / what it writes
- Reads nothing; it is the type the checks fill in.
- Every check writes its claim here rather than inventing a shape, so a consumer
  learns one vocabulary.

## The one law here
A finding carries its own evidence grade. A claim with no evidence behind it may
not be spelled the same way as one with a proof.
