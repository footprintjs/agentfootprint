**Fold** — bounded words from admitted tool coverage and absence declarations.

The projection reads the retained row at the exact occurrence witnessed by the reason. It preserves identical words from distinct registered tools, deduplicates repeat wording from the same tool, and counts statements beyond the shared budget. An oversized advice marker belongs to the advice field itself, so redacting that field hides its count along with its words. A period witness can supply an occurrence through the existing fold.

A visible `why` is read from the same retained item as its subject. Subjects keep their existing selection and spend the quote budget first. The remaining budget admits explanations in order. An earlier warning can be reconstructed with the same subjects and omission count.

## Declaration admission and union

These pure functions distinguish a result's declarations from its bytes. An
allowed replacement keeps recorded native coverage, absence and period limits,
and adds its own declarations with their own witness pointers. Equal words or
provider call IDs do not identify the same witness.

Admission reuses the owning occurrence's existing positive returned/delivery
proof. Withheld, unserved, failed, ambiguous and missing evidence grants no new
admission. Legacy history eviction retains only the existing native-period
attestation; it cannot invent coverage delivery proof.

Native declarations still count for the call's coverage check, but replacement
bytes are classified through their own door. Shape, unrelated argument and
validation witnesses keep their strict native-result boundary. No persisted
fact, parser, timer, retry or result-content reconstruction is added.
